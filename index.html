ABOUT NOOR AL-ISLAM

Learning Qur’an, Knowledge & Character

Noor Al-Islam is an online Islamic learning platform dedicated to helping children and learners build a strong connection with the Qur’an, develop Islamic knowledge, and grow with good character.

Who We Are

We provide structured online Islamic lessons from the comfort of home, with supportive tutors and a learning environment designed to help each student progress with confidence.

What We Offer

  • Qur’an Tilawah
  • Qur’an Tafseer
  • Tajweed
  • Islamic Studies

Our Mission

To make quality Islamic learning accessible, structured and engaging for learners through online education.

Our Vision

To nurture a generation that loves the Qur’an, understands their faith and carries Islamic knowledge and good character into everyday life.

Why Choose Noor Al-Islam

  • Structured online lessons
  • Qualified and supportive tutors
  • Small group classes
  • One-to-one learning available
  • Flexible online learning from home
  • Progress and attendance tracking
  • A focus on Qur’an, knowledge and character

HOW IT WORKS

A Simple Way to Begin Learning

Starting your Islamic learning journey with Noor Al-Islam is simple. Choose your learning option, complete your enquiry, and we will guide you through the next steps.

01۞

Choose Your Course

Choose from our four official courses:

  • Qur’an Tilawah
  • Qur’an Tafseer
  • Tajweed
  • Islamic Studies
02◈

Choose Your Learning Option

Group Classes — £8 per hour

One-to-One Classes — £12.50 per hour

All 4 Subjects Group Package — £110 per 4 weeks

Group classes have a maximum of 4 students, so every learner can receive support.
03✉

Send Your Enquiry

Complete the enquiry form with the learner’s details, preferred course and preferred learning arrangement.

Choose Start Your Enquiry to use our existing form, or contact the team directly:

Email Noor Al-IslamWhatsApp Noor Al-Islam

Begin Learning
